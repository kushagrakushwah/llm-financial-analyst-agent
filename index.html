Why pairing GLiNER with Qwen2.5-7B beats single-stage LLM prompting
Stage 1: Deterministic Extraction

GLiNER (DeBERTa-v3 Bidirectional Encoder)

Jointly encodes raw contract tokens with user-defined target labels. By calculating dot products between span vectors and label vectors, GLiNER captures exact numerical values, dates, and names in 118ms on CPU. Unlike generative models, it produces zero token hallucinations and returns exact start/end offsets.

Stage 2: Policy Reasoning

Qwen2.5-7B GRPO Policy Auditor

Receives the verified entity dictionary rather than unstructured long text, compressing input prompt size by over 70%. It calculates net exposure, checks for uncapped penalty liabilities, assesses governing jurisdiction risk, and formats structured compliance verdicts.
